# Pacing 5 — Intensity

Source: https://www.gdcreatorschool.com/docs/guides/gameplay-2/pacing-5-intensity/ — by TDP9, with
contributions by NotAModerator (GD Creator School, Intermediate Gameplay, Grade 2). Accessed 2026-10-09.

## Key points (paraphrased)
1. Intensity = strong emotion/tension, carried by visuals and gameplay; it should be **felt**, and
   feedback from others is the best judge (no fixed criteria, no numbers on the page).
2. **Levers:** movement (moving elements/icons, using different screen areas, sudden movements; mini
   portals add vertical chaos; mini cube/robot's smaller jumps suit calmer parts), pace (how quickly things
   happen; speed; high CPS keeps engagement), song choice (intensity comes from the song, not forced on it).
3. Intensity is relative to what came before: buildups lead into intense parts; **breaks between intense
   sections** ease overload; endings can be climactic or relaxing; follow the song's structure.
4. Overdoing movement/pace/chaos makes a level messy; chaos isn't automatically intensity; test.
5. Examples: Kinesiophobia (TheJosiahTurner), WOW (Trusta: builds into spam with breaks, full-circle
   ending), Nhelv (SirGuillester, Notlsa, Dienid: alternates intense and calm).

## Mapping to our tools
- Intensity proxies per section (speed, CPS, movement amplitude, effect density) are only **proxies**;

- Thermal Lock: drop 1 -> groove (break) -> build -> pre-drop dip -> drop 2 climax -> breakdown already
  includes the breaks the guide asks for; keep them.
