# Mechanics 5 — Limitations & Strategy

Source: https://www.gdcreatorschool.com/docs/guides/gameplay-2/mechanics-5-limitations-strategy/ — by
illusion2, with contributions by everedeck, psytrancegd, themilkcat_tmc (GD Creator School,
Intermediate Gameplay, Grade 2). Accessed 2026-10-09. Mostly general/platformer-oriented design.

## Key points (paraphrased)
1. **Limitations** make the player think; every mechanic needs clear boundaries.
2. **Lock & key:** fold requirements into mechanics (the mechanic is the key); chain locks for strategy;
   introduce chains early.
3. Reuse familiar elements in new contexts; one key can open two locks.
4. Strategic gameplay costs readability, which GD players value — playtest to separate a confusing
   mechanic (fix) from missing skill (accept).
5. Balance limits: harsh enough to guide, never fully stopping fun; partial restrictions beat total ones;
   don't reward inaction (cooldowns are weak unless time pressure exists).
6. Strategic freedom vs limits: gate optimization behind progress or skill.

## Mapping to our work
- Mostly relevant to platformer/mechanic levels; for Thermal Lock (classic) the useful parts are rule 3
  (reuse a motif in a new context: e.g. the drop-1 cube motif returns in drop 2 at a new speed/mode) and
  rule 4 (readability over strategy for a Hard-Harder classic level).

## How to verify
- Section spec marks motif reuse ("motif M1 from section 2, new context: 2x + upside-down").
