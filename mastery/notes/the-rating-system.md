# The Rating System (community guide)

Source: https://www.gdcreatorschool.com/docs/guides/main-skills/the-rating-system/ — by sparktwee,
with contributions by NotAModerator (GD Creator School, Main Skills, Grade 0). Accessed 2026-10-09.
It cites RobTop's GDRating.pdf and GDEditor guide and a community list of every rated level.
Read together with notes/robtop-rating.md (the primary source).

## Key points (paraphrased)
1. **Roles:** players, creators, moderators (Leaderboard / Standard / Elder; Standard and Elder send
   levels), and RobTop with final authority.
2. **Lifecycle:** unrated (NA) -> community difficulty votes -> peak attention, usually when rated ->
   decline (some rated, some famous, many never rated). An early creator has no reputation yet.
3. **Tiers** Star/Featured/Epic/Legendary/Mythic = 1-5 creator points; featured placement drives views.
4. **Getting seen:** moderator-run Discord request queues (some have notification roles; joining more
   servers helps), scheduled level-request streams (often random or bot queues; submit early), and DMs
   or in-game messages (slowest, often unchecked).
5. **Known:** popularity/stats don't decide a rate (reception can still create opportunities); rates
   aren't fully objective, and a moderator "sponsor" often advocates; flawed levels have been rated;
   timing is inconsistent.
6. **Unknown:** the exact criteria — the guide calls them subjective; there is no checklist or
   threshold.
7. **Improve chances:** build genuine relationships with moderators, follow each queue's rules, submit
   early in streams, be respectful (nobody owes a rate), understand RobTop's standards, keep the level
   public and verified.
8. Caution: don't make creator points the goal; unrated levels can still be worth making.

## Mapping to our work
- Confirms the mission rule: no invented tier criteria; standards come from corpus data and feedback.
- The feedback package (task 145) lists request servers/streams, but only ones verified current at

  beatable in his playtest (our sims don't count as verification).
