# Creating Details

Source: https://www.gdcreatorschool.com/docs/guides/deco-1/creating-details/ — by komatic5, with
contributions by sparktwee (GD Creator School, Basic Deco, Grade 1). Accessed 2026-10-09.

## Key rules (paraphrased)
1. A detail is an individual feature of an object; organize details deliberately or the result is messy.
2. **Purpose first:** every detail needs a reason (context: tells what an object is/does). Adding detail
   just to fill space or to look "rate-worthy" is risky.
3. Detail comes from **shape** (more segments, curves), **colour** (more colours, gradients) and
   **texture** (shape + colour; usually stylistic).
4. **Less is more / quality over quantity:** simpler work reads better, runs better on low-end hardware,
   stays recognizable. **Concentrate detail on the most important objects** (80/20: ~80% of the effect from
   ~20% of the work) — that creates focal points.
5. **Functional details** show what objects do (safe platforms, dangerous hazards, identifiable orbs);
   **narrative** details carry theme/story (attach them to a clear signifier); **aesthetic** details look
   good. Priority: functional/narrative first, aesthetic after — **aesthetic details are what LDM removes;
   functional ones stay**.
6. Process: idea -> many references -> extract one property from each -> combine and deviate -> polish
   with your own details. Studies (focused practice, dissecting levels) build a technique library.
